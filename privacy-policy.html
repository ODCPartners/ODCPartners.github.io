Privacy Policy

ODC Partners ("we," "us," "our") buys vacant land and contacts property owners about purchasing their property. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide: name, phone number, email address, and mailing address.
  • Property information: property address, parcel/APN number, and details you share about the property (access, taxes owed, liens, etc.).
  • Communications: records of calls, voicemails, and text messages between you and us, including transcripts used to respond to your inquiry.
  • Website information: basic technical data such as pages visited and device type.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your inquiries, evaluate properties for potential purchase, make and negotiate offers, complete transactions, and follow up on prior conversations. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing.

3. Text messaging (SMS)

If you call or text us at (661) 426-2857, you consent to receive text messages from ODC Partners in reply, including missed-call follow-ups and conversational messages about your property inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions for full SMS program terms.

4. We do not sell or share your mobile information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information — including your mobile phone number and SMS consent data — with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is used only for our own business communications with you, as described in this policy.

5. Service providers

We use trusted service providers to operate our business (for example, phone and messaging carriers and AI communication tools). These providers may process your information only to perform services for us and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. We keep records only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.
